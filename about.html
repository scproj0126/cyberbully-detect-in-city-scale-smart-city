Project Background

This project focuses on cyberbullying detection on online social platforms. With the rapid development of smart cities and the digital society, online communication has become an essential part of everyday life. However, aggressive language, harassment, and discriminatory expressions can negatively affect individuals’ mental well-being, community climate, and public safety.

Therefore, we aim to adopt data-driven methods to model and analyze potential bullying signals in online text, providing references for future research and system design.

Research Motivation

The motivation of this project is to explore the feasibility of machine learning methods for automated cyberbullying detection, and to analyze the performance and limitations of different models in real-world scenarios.

Through this project, we hope to deepen our understanding of digital safety in smart-city contexts and provide references for follow-up research or system design.
